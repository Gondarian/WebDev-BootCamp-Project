Addis Ababa University

Addis Ababa University(Amharic: አዲስ አበባ ዩኒቨርሲቲ), or AAU is an Ethiopian highest institute in Addis Ababa Ethiopia. The main campus is located in the Arada subcity,6killo area.

Currently,AAUAAU has enrolled more than 5000 undergraduate(under regular and continuing education program) and close to15000 postgraduate students in Applied Science, Social Science, Health science and Engineering and other fields.
